# Brightwater University — Undergraduate Transfer Admission Handbook

**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective for:** Fall 2026 transfer applicants

## Section 3: Academic Eligibility Requirements

### 3.1 Minimum Grade Point Average

Applicants must present a cumulative grade point average (GPA) of **2.75 or higher**
on a 4.0 scale, calculated across all transferable coursework, to be eligible for
regular admission review.

### 3.2 Required Coursework

All applicants must have successfully completed, with a grade of C or better, each
of the following courses or their documented equivalent:

- A first college-level mathematics course (e.g., Calculus I)
- A college-level English composition course

### 3.3 Course Withdrawals

Applicants with more than **two (2)** withdrawn courses (grade of "W") across their
submitted transcript history will be flagged for additional review rather than
receiving an automatic recommendation, regardless of GPA.

### 3.4 Minimum Transferable Credit

Applicants must have completed at least **24 transferable semester credits** at the
time of application.

### 3.5 Course Verification

All coursework listed on a submitted transcript must correspond to a recognized
course in Brightwater University's current course catalog, or be assessed as a
documented equivalent, before it may be counted toward the requirements in 3.1-3.4.

## Section 4: Decision Categories

- **Recommend Admit**: all requirements in 3.1-3.4 are met, and no unresolved course
  verification issues remain.
- **Recommend Deny**: cumulative GPA is below 2.75, or a required course in 3.2 was
  not completed with a passing grade.
- **Needs Additional Information**: withdrawal count exceeds the threshold in 3.3,
  one or more courses cannot be verified against the catalog, or the submitted
  transcript is incomplete or inconsistent.

All automated recommendations under this handbook are advisory only and require
review and sign-off by a human admissions officer before any applicant is notified
of a decision.
